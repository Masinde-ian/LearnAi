# AI for WhatsApp Business Replies

**Overview**

WhatsApp is where Kenyan customers already are — over 90% of internet users in Kenya are on it daily. This course shows you how to answer customer questions fast and professionally using AI, without sounding like a robot. By the end, you'll turn casual chats into sales and keep every customer feeling taken care of.

**Who this is for**

- Small business owners answering customer messages on one phone
- Solo sellers on Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook who sell via WhatsApp
- Salon, boutique, hardware, or food vendors losing sales to slow replies
- Anyone who freezes when a customer asks a tough question like "why so expensive?"

**What you'll be able to do**

- Write quick, professional replies to common customer questions
- Create saved reply templates for repeat questions using AI
- Handle objections about price, delivery, and trust politely
- Turn conversations into sales without sounding robotic

## Lesson 1: Set up your WhatsApp Business presence

The free WhatsApp Business app is enough for a solo seller. Download it, move your number over, and set these up before AI does any writing:

- Business name, category, and description
- A catalog with 2–5 products or services and prices
- Business hours and an away message for when you're busy

Why this matters: customers trust a business profile they can read. A full profile answers half your messages before they arrive. AI helps you write the description and product lines fast.

**Do this:** paste your business details into a prompt and ask for a 3-line description.

**Not this:** keep using a personal profile with no info, then wonder why buyers ask "are you legit?"

## Lesson 2: Write professional replies with AI

Most questions you get are the same ones every day: "price?", "delivery?", "still available?" Your job is to answer fast and warmly. AI drafts the wording; you add your flavour.

Give the AI context first — your business, your tone, your prices. Then it writes replies you can copy, edit, and send in seconds.

**Do this:** keep a note on your phone with your products, prices, delivery fee, and payment options (M-Pesa till or paybill number). Feed it into AI once, reuse the answers forever.

**Not this:** ask AI for a reply with no details. "Write a reply" gives you a generic answer that fits nobody.

## Lesson 3: Build saved reply templates

WhatsApp Business has a built-in feature called Quick Replies (saved replies). Type a short command like `/price` and a full reply appears instantly. This is where AI earns its keep.

Use AI to write templates for:

- Price inquiries and how to pay via M-Pesa
- Delivery options and costs (within town, upcountry via bus/courier)
- Product availability and sizes/colours
- After-hours or away messages

Keep each template short, warm, and specific to your business.

**Do this:** save one template per frequent question. Add a question mark or order button at the end of each so the conversation moves forward.

## Lesson 4: Handle objections politely

The three objections every Kenyan seller hears:

1. **"Too expensive"** — reframe value, offer a cheaper option, or suggest paying in two parts via M-Pesa.
2. **"How do I trust you?"** — share M-Pesa transaction history, offer video call/voice note proof, point to reviews, or offer pay-on-delivery where possible.
3. **"Delivery is too slow/costly"** — explain the courier, give a realistic window, or bundle delivery with a freebie.

AI writes the polite wording; you know the real facts. Never let AI invent prices or promises — only use facts you confirm.

**Do this:** ask AI to "respond to a price objection without being pushy" and keep your tone in the reply.

**Not this:** copy an AI reply that promises "free delivery worldwide" when you charge KES 200 within Nairobi.

## Lesson 5: Turn chats into sales without sounding robotic

The skill is closing politely. After answering the question, always move the chat forward with a gentle next step:

- "Would you like me to reserve this for you?"
- "Send your name and location and I'll calculate delivery."
- "I can take M-Pesa now — here's the till number."

Mix your own voice in. AI gives you the base; you add personality. Add a greeting in your name or a quick "asante" at the end. Customers buy from people, not chatbots.

**Do this:** after every reply, ask yourself "did I give the customer a clear next action?"

## Prompt Library

Copy, fill in the blanks, and adapt.

**Business profile description**

Use to set up or improve your WhatsApp Business "About" section.

```
Write a short WhatsApp Business "About" description for my [business type, e.g. cosmetics shop in Kisumu]. Keep it under 3 lines. Mention what I sell, delivery to [areas], and that I accept M-Pesa. Tone: friendly and professional.
```

**Quick reply: price inquiry**

Save this as a Quick Reply for "price?" messages.

```
Write a warm reply to a customer asking for the price of [product]. Price is KES [amount]. Include that payment is via M-Pesa till [number] and that delivery costs [amount] within [area]. End by asking where they'd like it delivered.
```

**Quick reply: product availability**

For "is it still available?" messages.

```
Reply to a customer asking if [product] is still available. It is in stock, available in [colours/sizes], and I can reserve it for 24 hours. Ask for their location to arrange delivery. Keep it short and friendly.
```

**Objection: "too expensive"**

Use to handle price pushback without sounding pushy.

```
A customer says my [product] at KES [price] is too expensive. Write a polite reply that explains what makes it worth the price [mention quality, durability, warranty], offers a cheaper alternative if I have one, and stays warm. Do not sound desperate or argumentative.
```

**Objection: "how do I know you're legit?"**

Use to build trust with a first-time buyer.

```
A new customer is worried about paying me online. Write a reply that builds trust: mention I have been selling for [X years], share that I can send a voice note or video of the product, and offer pay-on-delivery if I can. Keep it calm and confident, never defensive.
```

**Objection: delivery concerns**

Use when a customer worries about delivery time or cost.

```
A customer asks about delivery for [product] to [area]. Delivery costs KES [amount] and takes [X days] via [courier/bus]. Write a friendly reply explaining this and suggest collecting in person if they are in [your town]. End by asking how they would prefer to receive it.
```

**After-hours away message**

Set as your WhatsApp Business away message.

```
Write a WhatsApp Business away message for when I'm offline. Business hours are [e.g. 8am to 7pm]. Tell customers I'll reply in the morning, they can browse my catalog, and leave a message. Tone: warm and reassuring.
```

**Closing a sale politely**

Use at the end of any good conversation to push toward payment.

```
A customer has asked about [product] and seems interested. Write a friendly reply that confirms price [KES X], delivery [amount/area], and asks if they'd like to pay via M-Pesa till [number] to secure it. End with one clear question so the chat moves forward.
```

**Reply in your own voice**

Use when AI replies sound too formal or perfect.

```
Rewrite this reply in a warmer, more casual Kenyan voice — shorter sentences, like a small business owner chatting with a regular customer. Keep all facts (price, delivery, till number) exactly the same: [paste your reply]
```

**Weekly follow-up message**

Use to check on customers who asked but didn't buy.

```
Write a short WhatsApp follow-up for customers who asked about [product] last week but did not buy. Remind them it's still available, mention I can hold it for 24 hours, and ask if they have any questions. Do not sound desperate or spammy.
```

## Action plan

1. Set up the free WhatsApp Business app: profile, catalog, hours.
2. List your 5 most common customer questions.
3. Write AI replies for the top 3 using the prompts above.
4. Save them as Quick Replies with short commands.
5. Add an away message and a trust-building reply template.
6. Use AI replies for 3 real customer chats this week, editing them in your voice.
7. Note which replies got a positive response — keep those.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Letting AI invent prices, delivery fees, or promises — always check facts
- Copying replies word-for-word with no personal touch
- Writing long paragraphs customers won't read on a phone
- Ignoring the WhatsApp Business catalog and away message features
- Using the official WhatsApp Business API before you need it — it costs thousands monthly and is for scaling, not solo sellers
