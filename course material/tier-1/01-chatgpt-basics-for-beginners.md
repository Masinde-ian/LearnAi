# ChatGPT Basics for Beginners

## Overview

ChatGPT is the fastest way to get real work done without hiring anyone — emails, ideas, summaries, all in minutes. This course walks you through setting up a free account on your phone and writing your first useful prompts. By the end you will be using ChatGPT daily instead of staring at a blank screen.

## Who this is for

- Anyone who has heard about ChatGPT but has never used it
- Business owners who type their own M-Pesa confirmations and WhatsApp replies
- Students and job seekers who want faster answers
- Older learners who think AI is "for programmers" (it is not)

## What you'll be able to do

- Set up a free ChatGPT account on your phone in under 10 minutes
- Explain what ChatGPT can and cannot do
- Write clear prompts for emails, ideas, and summaries
- Spot AI mistakes and check information instead of trusting it blindly
- Use voice input so you don't have to type long prompts

## Lesson 1: What ChatGPT actually is

ChatGPT is a language model. Think of it as a very fast assistant that has read a huge amount of text and can predict helpful answers. You type a request, it replies.

It is **not** a search engine that always finds the latest news. It is not a calculator for exact numbers, and it is not a person. It has never "been" to your shop or your school. It knows a lot about the world up to its training cutoff, but it can be wrong.

Think of it like a bright friend who has read many books but has not lived your life. Give it good context and it helps a lot. Give it nothing and you get a vague answer.

The free tier handles everyday writing, research, and brainstorming well. You do not need to pay to start.

## Lesson 2: Setting up your free account on your phone

Most Kenyans access everything on a phone, and ChatGPT works best there too. Here is the walkthrough.

1. Open your browser or app store and search for "ChatGPT".
2. Download the official OpenAI app (free) or just use the website.
3. Tap **Sign up** and use your email or Google account.
4. Verify with the code they send you.
5. On first opening, you may be asked to log in. Do that and you are in.

The chat box is at the bottom of the screen. Tap it, type, and press send. You can also tap the microphone icon and speak instead of typing — great for busy hands.

You can use it free as much as you like. If you run into limits, wait a bit or use the free tier at a quieter time.

## Lesson 3: Writing your first 10 useful prompts

A prompt is just what you type. Short vague prompts give short vague answers. Good prompts have three parts: a role, a task, and a format.

Here are ten ready-to-use prompts for Kenyan life. Copy, fill in the blanks, and press send.

**Prompt 1 — A professional email**

```
Write a polite email to a client explaining that my order will be delivered
two days late because of transport delays. My business is a phone accessories
shop in Kisumu. Keep it short and warm, end with an apology.
```

**Prompt 2 — WhatsApp reply**

```
Reply to this customer message in a friendly tone: "Unauza hiyo charger
ya samsung?" I sell chargers at KES 500. Offer delivery within Nairobi.
```

**Prompt 3 — Summarise a message**

```
Summarise this long message in 3 bullet points: [paste the message]
```

**Prompt 4 — Social media post**

```
Write a Facebook post announcing that my tailor shop now does free
fittings on Saturdays. Address young professionals in Nakuru.
```

**Prompt 5 — Birthday wish**

```
Write a warm birthday WhatsApp message for my aunt. Mention that I am
grateful she raised me. Keep it under 100 words, not too formal.
```

**Prompt 6 — Name ideas**

```
Give me 10 names for a small salon in Eldoret that also sells beauty
products. Mix English and Kiswahili names.
```

**Prompt 7 — Study help**

```
Explain what inflation is in simple terms, like you are teaching a
Form 2 student. Give one Kenyan example.
```

**Prompt 8 — Rewrite for clarity**

```
Rewrite this sentence to be clearer and shorter: [paste your sentence]
```

**Prompt 9 — Checklist**

```
Turn these tasks into a morning checklist: [list your tasks]
```

**Prompt 10 — Problem solving**

```
My small grocery shop is losing customers to a bigger shop nearby.
Give me 5 practical ideas to win them back on a small budget.
```

## Lesson 4: Common beginner mistakes

**Vague prompts.** "Help me" gives a useless reply. Give a role, a task, and a format every time.

**Trusting everything blindly.** ChatGPT can be confident and wrong. It is a tool, not a source of truth. Double-check facts, prices, and legal matters.

**Asking for personal data.** It does not know your M-Pesa balance, your customers, or your exam results. Give it the details instead.

**Using it for sensitive things without care.** Do not paste bank PINs or ID numbers into any AI chat.

**Giving up after one answer.** If the first reply misses, say "That's not quite what I meant, try again with..." and refine it.

**Treating it as a search engine.** For fresh news, use a search tool. For writing, thinking, and drafting, use ChatGPT.

## Prompt Library

Each prompt below is ready to copy. Fill in the blanks marked with brackets.

**Write a product description**

```
Write a short product description for a small backpack I sell for KES 1,200.
Mention it is water resistant and good for university students in Kenya.
Keep it under 60 words.
```

**Draft a follow-up message**

```
A customer asked about my prices on WhatsApp yesterday but has not replied.
Write a polite follow-up message asking if they are still interested.
```

**Plan a simple day**

```
I have these 4 tasks today: [tasks]. Arrange them in order and suggest
what time I should do each one.
```

**Explain something simply**

```
Explain [topic] in three sentences a 13-year-old in Kenya would understand.
```

**Turn notes into a paragraph**

```
Turn these rough notes into one clean paragraph: [paste notes]
```

**Translate to Kiswahili**

```
Translate this message into natural Kiswahili, not too formal:
[paste message]
```

**Improve my grammar**

```
Fix the grammar in this message but keep my voice:
[paste message]
```

**Brainstorm ideas**

```
Give me 8 ideas to [goal]. Pick the 3 best and explain why.
```

**Write a short advert**

```
Write a 40-word WhatsApp status advert for [your product/service].
End with "Order via WhatsApp and pay with M-Pesa".
```

**Summarise a long document**

```

[paste the text]
```

## Action plan

1. Create your ChatGPT account today, on your phone.
2. Send your first message: ask it to explain something you deal with daily.
3. Try all 10 prompts from Lesson 3 this week.
4. Pick one real task (an email or a post) and do it fully with AI.
5. Rewrite the AI answer in your own words before sending it anywhere.
6. Save your favourite prompts in your phone notes for reuse.

- Believing everything AI says without checking
- Pasting passwords or ID numbers into chat
- Using ChatGPT for the latest news and getting stale answers
- Giving up after one bad answer instead of re-prompting
