# AI Voice & Video Tools Basics

**Overview**

You can now make a captioned, voiced short video without ever showing your face or recording your voice. This course walks you through the free AI tools — CapCut, subtitle generators, and AI voiceover — and shows you how to turn a simple script into a short video for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

**Who this is for**

- Aspiring content creators who are shy on camera
- Small business owners who want product videos without filming themselves
- Anyone who wants to post short videos but finds editing intimidating

**What you'll be able to do**

- Turn a script into a captioned short video in under an hour
- Generate a professional voiceover without recording yourself
- Add auto-captions and export for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts
- Know which AI tools are free and which upgrades are worth paying for

## Lesson 1: The free tool kit in 2026

You don't need a studio. This is the free stack:

- **CapCut** — free video editor with built-in AI captions, auto-subtitles, and text-to-video
- **ElevenLabs** — free tier for realistic AI voiceover
- **Edge TTS / TikTok TTS** — free voiceover alternatives built into some tools
- **ReCap** — free subtitle/caption generator option

All have free tiers that cover a beginner's needs. Start free; pay only when you're growing and need better voices or editing.

**Do this:** install CapCut and test one AI caption on a 30-second clip today.

**Not this:** paying for subscriptions before you've posted your first five videos.

## Lesson 2: Turn a script into a short video

Short videos follow a simple structure:

1. **Hook (0–3 seconds)** — one line that stops the scroll.
2. **Body (10–30 seconds)** — the main point, one idea only.
3. **Call to action (last 5 seconds)** — "follow for more" or "share this."

Write the script first — AI helps with this. Then record it, or paste it into a voiceover tool, and bring it into CapCut.

**Do this:** keep scripts under 60 seconds (around 120–150 words) so they fit Shorts/Reels/TikTok limits.

**Not this:** one video covering three topics. One video, one idea, every time.

## Lesson 3: Generate a voiceover without recording

If you don't want to record your own voice, AI reads your script for you.

- **ElevenLabs (free tier)** — paste your script, pick a voice, download audio
- **Edge TTS** — free, often used for clean simple narration
- **CapCut text-to-speech** — built-in, quick for short clips

**Important:** you still write the script and choose the tone. AI just reads it. Pick a voice that matches your content — calm for finance tips, energetic for entertainment.

**Do this:** listen to your generated voiceover once and cut any awkward pauses or repeated words before exporting.

**Not this:** letting AI voiceovers run without a script — rambling audio loses viewers fast.

## Lesson 4: Captions, export, and platforms

Captions are not optional in 2026 — most people watch muted.

- CapCut auto-generates subtitles in seconds. Fix any spelling mistakes, especially names and place names.
- Keep captions short, centre, and readable on a small phone screen.
- Export at **9:16 (vertical)** for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.

**Do this:** export vertical, keep the video under 60 seconds, and add one clear on-screen call to action.

**Not this:** exporting 16:9 horizontal video for TikTok — it looks tiny in the feed.

## Prompt Library

Copy, fill in the blanks, and adapt.

**Short video script from an idea**

Use to turn one idea into a complete script with hook, body, and call to action.

```
Write a short video script (under 60 seconds, about 130 words) on [topic, e.g. how to save money on airtime in Kenya]. Start with a strong hook for a Kenyan audience, keep it conversational, end with a call to action. No emojis.
```

**5 hooks for one topic**

Use to test different opening lines for the same video.

```
Give me 5 different opening lines (hooks) for a short video about [topic]. Each hook must be under 10 words and make someone stop scrolling. Write them for a Kenyan phone-screen audience.
```

**Voiceover script for a product**

Use when selling one product in a video.

```
Write a 40-second voiceover script promoting [product, e.g. handmade batik bags from Nairobi]. Mention the price range, that delivery is available countrywide via courier, and that I accept M-Pesa. End with a clear call to action.
```

**Fun fact / educational reel script**

Use for faceless educational content.

```
Write a 30-second educational reel script on [fact/topic, e.g. why mangoes ripen after being picked]. Simple language, one fact only, ends with "follow for more." Suited for an AI voiceover.
```

**Caption text for the video**

Use to pair a caption with your video when posting.

```
Write a short caption for a TikTok/Reel about [topic]. Max 2 lines. Include 3 relevant hashtags. Friendly Kenyan tone, no emojis.
```

**AI voiceover tone check**

Use before generating the voiceover.

```
I will paste a script for an AI voiceover. Suggest the best voice style (calm, energetic, warm) and speaking pace for content about [topic] aimed at [audience, e.g. Kenyan students]. Then I will paste the script.
```

**Turn a long post into a short script**

Use to repurpose an existing post into a video.

```
Turn this blog/WhatsApp post into a 45-second short video script with a hook, body, and call to action: [paste your post]
```

**Fix a clunky script**

Use when your script sounds stiff.

```
Rewrite this video script so it sounds like a Kenyan speaking casually to a friend. Shorter sentences, less formal. Keep all facts the same: [paste script]
```

**Faceless video: text-to-video scene list**

Use in CapCut's text-to-video or when picking stock clips.

```
I'm making a faceless video about [topic]. Suggest 6 simple visual scenes I can film or create with my phone (no face required), in order, that match the script: [paste script]
```

**Posting checklist**

Use before you hit post.

```
Give me a checklist for posting a short video on TikTok/Reels/Shorts: resolution, aspect ratio, caption length, hashtags, and best time to post for a Kenyan audience. Keep it short.
```

## Action plan

1. Install CapCut and open the free version.
2. Pick one topic and write a script using the prompts above.
3. Generate a voiceover with ElevenLabs or CapCut's text-to-speech.
4. Add the audio to CapCut and generate auto-captions.
5. Fix captions, export vertical at 9:16.
6. Post it on one platform with a caption.
7. Watch the first 3 seconds back — if the hook is weak, change it next time.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Making videos longer than 60 seconds for Shorts/Reels/TikTok
- Exporting horizontal video for vertical platforms
- Using AI voiceover with no script — it sounds rambling
- Ignoring auto-captions and posting without subtitles
- Paying for subscriptions before posting your first videos free
- On YouTube, forgetting to tick "altered/synthetic content" when you use AI voices — you risk strikes
