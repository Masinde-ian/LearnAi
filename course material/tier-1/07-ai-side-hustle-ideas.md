# Using AI to Find a Side Hustle Idea

## Overview

You have skills. You have a phone. You probably have more free time than you think. This course uses AI to turn that into a side hustle — brainstorming ideas matched to you, validating them before you spend money, and writing a one-page plan. Real Kenyan examples included, from virtual assistance to selling digital products via M-Pesa.

## Who this is for

- Salaried people who want extra income beside their job
- Students who want to earn from their phone
- Anyone with a skill who has never considered selling it
- People who tried a hustle and lost money on a bad idea

- Brainstorm side hustle ideas matched to your skills and schedule
- Validate an idea before spending any money
- Write a one-page plan with AI's help
- Spot the difference between a real hustle and a dream

## Lesson 1: Brainstorming ideas from your skills

Do not start with "what can I do?" Start with what you already know. Your job, your hobbies, your problems, your neighbourhood — all of it is raw material.

Give AI a full picture:

```
You are a business coach in Kenya. Based on my skills [skills],
my time [hours per week], my money for starting [amount], and
my location [town], suggest 10 side hustles I could start on
my phone. Rank them by how realistic they are for me.
```

The best ideas usually sit at the crossing of three things: something you can do, something people pay for, and something you can start small. AI helps you see all the combinations you were missing.

## Lesson 2: Validating before you spend money

Most failed hustles fail because nobody checked if anyone would pay. Validation is cheap and fast — do it before buying equipment or running ads.

- Ask real people: friends, family, your WhatsApp groups. "Would you pay KES 300 for this?"
- Search for proof: is anyone already selling this in Kenya? Check Jiji, Instagram, and TikTok. Competition proves demand.
- Sell before you build: tell people you offer the service, take an order, then deliver. This is the cheapest test of all.

AI helps you draft the questions and interpret the answers:

```
Here is my hustle idea: [idea]. Write 5 questions I can ask
10 people to validate it, and tell me what answers mean it
is worth pursuing.
```

## Lesson 3: Writing a one-page plan with AI

You do not need a 40-page business plan. One page is enough to keep you honest.

```
Write a one-page plan for my hustle [idea]. Include: the customer,
the offer, how I will reach customers, my starting costs, my price,
how I get paid, and my first 3 steps this month. Kenyan context.
```

Keep the plan simple: who you serve, what you charge, how they pay, how you deliver. Update it monthly. A one-page plan you actually follow beats a thick document that gathers dust.

## Lesson 4: Real small AI-assisted hustles in Kenya

These are realistic, phone-first, and can start with money you already have.

**Virtual assistant (VA).** Help a small business with their inbox, WhatsApp replies, or social posts. AI drafts, you deliver. Clients pay via M-Pesa or PayPal.

**Transcription.** Turn audio and meetings into text. Use free AI transcription tools like Otter or Whisper to speed up, then deliver clean transcripts. Sell on Fiverr, Upwork, or to local podcasters.

**Caption writing.** You learned it in the captions course — now sell it. Local businesses need weekly captions and do not want to write them. Package: "10 captions a week for KES 500".

**Digital products.** A printable planner, a price list template, a study notes pack — created once, sold many times, paid via M-Pesa. Small but repeatable.

**Graphic posts with Canva.** Small businesses need flyers and posts. One good design system, reused, earns per order.

All of these share one pattern: a skill AI helps you do faster, sold to someone who does not have time.

## Lesson 5: Start small, get paid, repeat

The first hustle that makes money is rarely the last. Start with the smallest version that can produce a paying customer this month.

- Set a tiny starting budget — KES 0 to a few thousand.
- Use M-Pesa as your till: Lipa Na M-Pesa or a simple till number for customers.
- Get the first 3 customers before you scale anything.
- Reinvest what you make instead of spending it all.

Side hustles fail from over-planning and under-starting. Fix that by shipping the smallest offer, collecting payment, and doing it again.

## Prompt Library

Copy, fill in the brackets, and adapt.

**Idea brainstorm**

```
You are a Kenyan business coach. Suggest 10 phone-based side
hustles for someone with these skills [skills], free time
[hours/week], starting budget [amount], located in [town].
Rank by realism.
```

**Validate my idea**

```
I want to start [idea]. Write 5 questions to ask 10 potential
customers to validate it, and explain what answers mean I
should continue or stop.
```

**Who is my customer**

```
Describe the exact customer who would pay for [service].
Include age, town, income level, and where I can find them
online in Kenya.
```

**One-page plan**

```
Write a one-page plan for [idea]. Include customer, offer,
how I reach customers, starting costs, price, payment method,
and my first 3 steps this month.
```

**Price check**

```
I plan to charge KES [amount] for [service]. Is that fair for
Kenya? Suggest a price range and a cheap way to test it.
```

**M-Pesa payment setup**

```
Explain how I should set up payments for my phone-based hustle
using M-Pesa: a till number, paybill, or Lipa Na M-Pesa. Which
suits a solo seller?
```

**Offer to clients**

```
Write a WhatsApp message offering [service] to [type of client].
Include my price and how to pay via M-Pesa. Friendly, not pushy.
```

**First 3 customers**

```
Give me a simple plan to get my first 3 customers for [service]
this month, using WhatsApp, my network, and one social platform.
```

**Digital product idea**

```
Suggest 5 digital products I can create once and sell for
KES 200–1000 each, based on my knowledge of [topic].
```

**Weekly plan**

```
Make me a weekly schedule that protects my main job and gives
[hours] to my side hustle. Keep it realistic.
```

## Action plan

1. List your skills, free hours, and starting money honestly.
2. Run the idea brainstorm prompt and pick one realistic idea.
3. Validate it with real people using the validation prompt.
4. Write a one-page plan with AI.
5. Set up your M-Pesa payment method.
6. Get your first 3 customers this month — however small.
7. Review what worked and repeat it.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Buying equipment and running ads before validating
- Choosing a hustle you have no skill or interest in
- Copying a hustle with no plan for who your customer is
- Waiting for the "perfect" idea instead of starting
- Spending everything you earn before the hustle grows
