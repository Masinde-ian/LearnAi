# Claude Basics for Beginners

Claude is the AI assistant that reads, writes, and thinks like a sharp colleague — and it is completely free to start. ## Lesson 1: What Claude is and why it matters

Claude is made by Anthropic, a company focused on building AI that is helpful, harmless, and honest. It is a language model, just like ChatGPT, but it has a few things that make it stand out.

First, Claude reads very long documents. While ChatGPT has limits on how much text you can paste at once, Claude can handle entire contracts, research papers, or book chapters in a single conversation. This makes it a powerful reading and analysis tool.

Second, Claude writes in a more natural, human tone. Ask it to write an email or a social media post and the result sounds less robotic than most AI tools. For Kenyan business owners who need professional but warm communication, this matters.

Third, Claude has a feature called Projects. Think of a Project as a folder where you keep all the files and instructions for one task — a business plan, a research project, a content calendar. Claude remembers everything inside that folder across multiple conversations. You do not have to re-explain yourself each time.

The free tier gives you enough to work with every day. You do not need to pay to get real value.

## Lesson 2: Setting up your free account on your phone

Claude works well on both phones and computers. Here is how to get started on your phone.

1. Open your phone browser and go to claude.ai
2. Tap **Sign up** and enter your email address or use your Google account
3. You will receive a verification code — enter it
4. Choose a display name (your real name or a nickname)
5. You are in. The chat box is at the bottom of the screen

If you prefer an app, search for "Claude" in your phone app store and download the official Anthropic app. The experience is the same.

On first use, Claude will introduce itself. You can immediately start typing or tap one of the suggested prompts to see how it works. Try saying: "Explain what you can do in 5 bullet points." You will get a clear overview in seconds.

To use voice input instead of typing, tap the microphone icon in the chat box and speak your prompt. This is useful when your hands are busy or you want to give a longer instruction without tapping on a small screen.

You can also upload images to Claude. Take a photo of a document, a receipt, or a whiteboard and ask Claude to read, explain, or summarise it. This works on the free tier.

## Lesson 3: How Claude differs from ChatGPT

Both tools answer questions and write text. The differences matter when you know what to look for.

**Reading long documents.** Claude can read and analyse much longer pieces of text in one go. If you have a 20-page contract or a long school assignment, paste the whole thing and ask Claude to summarise it, find specific clauses, or explain the tricky parts. ChatGPT often asks you to break long documents into smaller chunks.

**Writing quality.** Claude tends to write more naturally and with better flow. For business emails, social media posts, reports, and any writing that needs to sound human, Claude often produces a better first draft.

**Projects.** Claude lets you create a Project — a dedicated space where you upload files, set instructions, and have multiple conversations that all share the same context. This is powerful for ongoing work like a business plan or a content strategy.

**Personality.** Claude is conversational but professional. It does not use emojis unless you ask, and it tends to give direct, well-structured answers without unnecessary fluff.

**When to use ChatGPT instead.** ChatGPT has stronger integrations with plugins, browsing, and image generation. For quick research with live web results, or for generating images, ChatGPT may be the better pick. For deep reading, polished writing, and ongoing projects, Claude excels.

The best approach: use both. They are free and each has strengths.

## Lesson 4: Writing your first 10 effective prompts

A prompt is what you type into Claude. The better your prompt, the better the answer. Claude responds well to clear instructions, context about your situation, and a specific format for the output.

Here are ten prompts you can copy, fill in the blanks, and use today.

**Prompt 1 — Professional email**

```
Write a polite email to a client explaining that my order will be
delivered two days late because of transport delays. My business is
a phone accessories shop in Kisumu. Keep it short and warm, end
with an apology.
```

**Prompt 2 — WhatsApp customer reply**

```
Reply to this customer message in a friendly but professional tone:
"Uko na hiyo smartwatch ya Amazfit?" I sell smartwatches at KES
3,500. Offer delivery within Nairobi via Glovo or boda.
```

**Prompt 3 — Summarise a long message**

```
Summarise this message in 5 clear bullet points. Keep the key facts
and numbers: [paste the message]
```

**Prompt 4 — Social media post**

```
Write a Facebook post announcing that my tailor shop now offers
free fittings on Saturdays. Target young professionals in Nakuru.
Keep it under 80 words, friendly and direct.
```

**Prompt 5 — Business proposal help**

```
I run a small cleaning company in Nairobi. Write a short proposal
for a one-time deep clean of a 3-bedroom house. Include what is
included, the price (KES 4,500), and how to book. Keep it
professional but warm.
```

**Prompt 6 — Study explanation**

```
Explain what supply and demand means in simple terms, like you are
teaching a Form 2 student. Give one example using maize prices
in Kenya.
```

**Prompt 7 — Name ideas**

```
Give me 10 name ideas for a small coffee shop in Thika that also
sells pastries. Mix English and Kiswahili names. Explain the
meaning of each.
```

**Prompt 8 — Rewrite for clarity**

```
Rewrite this sentence to be clearer and more professional:
[paste your sentence]
```

**Prompt 9 — Product description**

```
Write a short product description for a wireless Bluetooth speaker
I sell for KES 1,800. It is waterproof, has 12-hour battery, and
is good for outdoor events. Keep it under 60 words.
```

**Prompt 10 — Problem solving**

```
My small grocery shop is losing customers to a bigger shop that
opened nearby. Give me 5 practical, low-budget ideas to win them
back. Focus on what a small shop can do that a big one cannot.
```

## Lesson 5: Using Claude Projects for ongoing work

Projects are one of Claude's best features. A Project is a dedicated workspace where you can upload files, set instructions, and have multiple conversations that all share the same context.

Here is how to create your first Project:

1. On the left sidebar, tap or click **Projects**
2. Tap **Create Project**
3. Give it a name — for example, "My Business Plan" or "School Research"
4. Add instructions — tell Claude what this project is about and how it should behave. For example: "You are my business advisor. All advice should be practical for a small shop in Mombasa. Use KES for all money."
5. Upload any files you want Claude to read — contracts, notes, spreadsheets, PDFs
6. Start a conversation inside the Project

Now every conversation inside that Project has access to the files and instructions you set. You do not need to re-explain your business or re-upload your documents each time.

Good Projects to create right now:

- **Business docs:** Upload your price list, product catalogue, or business registration papers. Ask Claude to draft proposals, emails, or social posts that match your actual business.
- **School research:** Upload assignment briefs, reading materials, or lecture notes. Ask Claude to explain concepts, summarise chapters, or help you write essays.
- **Content planning:** Upload your content calendar or past social media posts. Ask Claude to plan next week's content in your brand voice.

## Lesson 6: Common beginner mistakes

**Vague prompts.** Typing "help me" gives a vague answer. Always include what you want, for whom, and in what format. "Write a WhatsApp reply to a customer asking about prices, friendly tone, under 50 words" works far better.

**Not pasting enough context.** Claude excels when you give it the raw material. Paste the email you need to reply to, the message you need to summarise, the contract you need explained. The more context, the better the output.

**Ignoring Projects.** Many beginners use Claude as a one-shot chatbot. Projects are where the real power is. Create a Project for any ongoing task and watch the quality of responses improve dramatically.

**Trusting everything blindly.** Claude can sound very confident while being wrong. It is a tool, not a source of truth. Check facts, verify numbers, and consult professionals for legal or medical matters.

**Giving up after one answer.** If the first draft is not right, tell Claude what to change. "Make it shorter," "add a greeting," "use a more professional tone" — one follow-up message often gets you exactly what you need.

**Using it for things it cannot do.** Claude does not know your M-Pesa balance, your school exam timetable, or the latest news. Give it the information it needs instead of assuming it knows.
