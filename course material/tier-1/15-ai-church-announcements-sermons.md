# AI for Church Announcements & Sermon Notes

**Overview** — This course helps you turn rough sermon notes into clear, shareable summaries, write weekly church announcements and WhatsApp group messages, and make simple graphics for church events with AI and Canva. The one clear win: your church communications take one hour a week, not a whole afternoon.

**Who this is for**

- Secretaries, pastors, and youth leaders who post church announcements weekly
- Volunteer media or social media people in a local church
- Pastors who want to share sermon summaries on YouTube, WhatsApp, or Telegram
- Anyone whose WhatsApp group messages get ignored because they are too long

**What you'll be able to do**

- Turn rough sermon notes into clean summaries in minutes
- Write short, clear announcements and WhatsApp messages people actually read
- Create simple event graphics with AI + Canva
- Keep the pastor's voice and message authentic while AI does the drafting

## Lessons

### Lesson 1: From rough notes to a shareable summary

Sermon notes are often messy — bullet fragments, phone notes, random verses. Give the whole thing to AI and ask it to organise it without inventing anything. Ask for a structure: the main verse, the key point, three supporting points, and a takeaway. You can then post the summary on the church WhatsApp group, share it on Telegram, or use it as the basis for a YouTube video description.

**Do this:** Feed AI the raw notes and say "organise these, keep my words, do not add ideas."

**Not this:** Ask AI to "write a sermon about faith" — that creates a new sermon, not a summary of yours.

### Lesson 2: Announcements people actually read

The biggest mistake is the long, everything-in-one paragraph. Fix it with a simple formula: one clear message per announcement, three lines max, and a call to action. Ask AI to rewrite your announcements to fit a phone screen. Use a standard order in your WhatsApp group — "Good morning family. Three things this week:" — then numbered items. Short messages get read; long ones get skipped.

### Lesson 3: Simple event graphics with AI + Canva

For fundraisers, crusades, or choir performances, you do not need a designer. Generate a short description and event details with AI, then build the graphic in Canva:

1. Open Canva and pick a template under "Church" or "Flyers"
2. Paste your AI-written text (event, date, time, venue, contact)
3. Add a simple background — solid colour or a single photo works best
4. Add the till number or M-Pesa paybill for the offering or tickets
5. Download as PNG and share on WhatsApp

Keep it to one or two colours and one font. Clean beats fancy.

### Lesson 4: Respectful AI use that keeps the message authentic

AI is an assistant, not the preacher. The message, stories, and heart come from you. Use AI to polish and organise, but keep your own testimony and illustrations in your own words. Do not let AI generate a whole sermon with no human review. If a summary changes the meaning, edit it. Many churches have had public embarrassment from posting AI text that missed the point — always read aloud before posting.

### Lesson 5: A weekly content routine

Set one recurring hour — say Monday evening after work. In that hour: paste the Sunday notes into AI for a summary, write that week's announcements, and make the event graphic. Schedule the WhatsApp posts. This turns scattered weekly chaos into a 60-minute routine, and it works the same whether you are the pastor, the secretary, or the media volunteer.

## Prompt Library

**Sermon summary**

**How to use it:** Paste your rough notes after the prompt.

```
Here are my rough sermon notes. Organise them into a shareable summary for our church WhatsApp group: the main verse, the core message, three short points, and one takeaway action. Keep my wording as much as possible, do not add new ideas. Notes: [paste notes]
```

**Short announcement**

**How to use it:** List your announcements and get a clean version.

```
Rewrite these church announcements for WhatsApp. Keep each one to 3 lines, phone-friendly, with a clear call to action and the contact. Use a numbering style. Announcements: [list them]
```

**Announcement opener**

**How to use it:** Warm, consistent start for your group messages.

```
Write a short, warm opener for our church WhatsApp group for this [Sunday]. Mention thankfulness for the week, then list 3 upcoming items: [items]. Keep it under 50 words, no emojis.
```

**Event graphic text**

**How to use it:** Get copy for a Canva flyer.

```
Write the text for a Canva flyer for a [type of event, e.g. church fundraiser] at [venue] on [date/time]. Include: title, one line on the purpose, date, time, venue, contact, and M-Pesa till number placeholder. Short, bold, phone-readable.
```

**Canva layout ideas**

**How to use it:** Before opening Canva, get direction.

```
Give me 3 simple Canva flyer layout ideas for a [event]. For each: colour scheme, where text goes, and what image or shape to use. Prefer clean designs that work on WhatsApp and Instagram.
```

**YouTube video description**

**How to use it:** For sermons shared on YouTube.

```
Turn this sermon summary into a YouTube video description: title ideas, 3 bullet points of what the viewer will learn, and a 2-line closing inviting people to join us or give. Keep it simple. Summary: [paste summary]
```

**Pastor's voice check**

**How to use it:** Make sure the summary sounds like you.

```
Read this summary and rewrite it to sound like a warm Kenyan pastor speaking — direct, personal, and simple English. Keep the meaning and Bible verses unchanged. Summary: [paste]
```

**Weekly planner**

**How to use it:** Plan church communication for the week.

```
I look after our church's weekly communication. Plan a simple schedule for this week: one WhatsApp announcement day, one sermon summary post, one event reminder. Include the best days/times for evening phone use. Keep it to 6 lines.
```

**Bible study discussion questions**

**How to use it:** For small groups after a sermon.

```
Based on this passage [verse], write 4 short discussion questions for our midweek Bible study, in simple English, plus one practical challenge for the week. Passage: [verse or summary]
```

**Avoid-the-long-post rewrite**

**How to use it:** If you already wrote something too long.

```
Make this church notice shorter for WhatsApp. Maximum 4 lines, keep the key info (what, when, where, who to call), and tell me what to delete if needed. Notice: [paste]
```

2. Rewrite this week's announcements with the "3 lines max" rule.
3. Make one event graphic in Canva and share it on WhatsApp.
4. Read every AI output aloud before posting.
5. Set one recurring hour for church communication.
6. Save your best prompts in your phone's notes.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Asking AI to "write a sermon" and posting it unedited
- Long paragraphs that nobody reads on a phone
- Graphics crammed with text, colours, and fonts
- Posting AI text without reading it aloud first
- Forgetting the call to action (contact or till number)
- Posting announcements at random times instead of on a schedule
