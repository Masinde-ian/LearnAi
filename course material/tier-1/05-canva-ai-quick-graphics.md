# Canva + AI for Quick Graphics

## Overview

You do not need a designer to make posters, flyers, and social posts. Canva's built-in AI — Magic Design, Magic Write, and the background remover — turns your ideas into finished graphics in minutes. This course walks you through each tool, shows you what is actually worth paying for, and leaves you with 5 reusable templates for any business.

## Who this is for

- Small business owners posting for themselves
- Anyone tired of ugly text-on-photo graphics
- Students and church/community groups making event posters
- Future freelancers who want to offer design as a service

## What you'll be able to do

- Use Magic Design to generate layouts from a description
- Remove photo backgrounds in one click
- Write and place copy fast with Magic Write
- Create posters, flyers, and social posts in minutes
- Tell free vs paid Canva features apart and spend only where it matters
- Reuse 5 templates for any business

## Lesson 1: Getting started on your phone

Canva works beautifully on a phone screen. Download the app (free) or use canva.com on your browser. Sign up with email or Google.

The home screen shows **Templates**, **Projects**, and the search bar. Search "poster", "flyer", "Instagram post", or "price list" and hundreds of free, Kenya-ready designs appear. Tap a template, edit the text and colours, and you are done.

Design on phone is fine for most jobs. Use the desktop website for heavy multi-page work.

## Lesson 2: Magic Design — graphics from a description

Magic Design is Canva's AI that builds a design from words. Open the app, tap **Magic Design** (or start a design and select it), and type something like:

"Poster for a Nairobi hair salon, new customer discount, black and gold, modern."

It returns several layouts. Pick one, swap in your business name and phone number, and export. This is the fastest route from idea to finished graphic anywhere.

The free tier includes a limited number of Magic Design credits each month (roughly a couple of hundred standard uses). Paid plans lift the limit and unlock more power — but free is genuinely enough to start.

## Lesson 3: Magic Write and the background remover

**Magic Write** is Canva's AI text tool. Use it to draft the words that go on your graphic: headlines, taglines, and short ad copy. Type your request, get text, then adjust.

**Background remover** does exactly what the name says — one tap strips the background from a product or person photo. This is the tool that makes your product shots look professional, and it is the single most useful AI feature for sellers.

The background remover is a paid feature on Canva, but the free trial and the occasional free credit let you test it. For regular use, you can also use free browser tools for simple background removal and keep Canva paid only if it pays for itself.

## Lesson 4: Free vs paid — what is actually worth it

Canva's free tier covers templates, basic editing, and a limited amount of AI credits. Paid Canva Pro unlocks unlimited AI credits, the background remover, brand kits, and premium content.

Before paying, ask: will this make me money? For a shop posting flyers, free is usually enough. For someone selling design services, Pro pays for itself quickly.

The rule: master the free tier first. Only upgrade when a specific paid feature — usually the background remover or unlimited AI — is blocking work you are being paid for.

## Lesson 5: 5 reusable templates for any business

Save these five as your starter kit. Duplicate and edit instead of starting from scratch.

1. **Price list** — clean table, your items, prices, "Pay via M-Pesa" at the bottom.
2. **Sale/post flyer** — big discount, dates, WhatsApp number.
3. **Social post** — square, brand colours, headline, CTA.
4. **Business card** — name, services, phone, location.
5. **WhatsApp Status graphic** — tall and narrow, short message, clear CTA.

Keep brand colours and font consistent across all five and your business starts to look established — a huge trust boost on WhatsApp.

## Prompt Library

Copy, fill in the brackets, and adapt.

**Magic Design poster**

```
Poster for [business] announcing [offer]. Colours: [colours].
Style: [modern/bold/minimal]. Include a place for a phone number.
```

**Magic Design social post**

```
Instagram post for [business], topic [topic]. Square format.
Colours: [colours]. Keep text short with a call to action.
```

**Magic Write headline**

```
Write 3 short headlines for a flyer about [offer]. Under 6 words
each. Bold and friendly, no emojis.
```

**Magic Write ad copy**

```
Write 2 lines of ad copy for a poster promoting [service] to
customers in [town]. End with "Order via WhatsApp".
```

**Price list copy**

```
Help me structure a price list for: [items and prices].
Organise it clearly and add a "Pay via M-Pesa" line.
```

**WhatsApp Status graphic**

```
Design a tall graphic for WhatsApp Status promoting [offer].
Short text, one clear call to action, brand colour [colour].
```

**Product post idea**

```
Suggest 3 poster ideas for my [product]. For each: the headline,
the main image description, and the call to action.
```

**Font pairing**

```
Suggest a font pairing for a [business] brand: one for headings,
one for body text. Professional and readable on phone screens.
```

**Colour palette**

```
Give me a 3-colour palette for a [business] targeting [audience].
Describe where each colour should be used.
```

**Posting plan**

```
Plan a week of simple Canva posts for [business]: one topic and
graphic idea per day. Keep them easy to make in 15 minutes.
```

- Using too many fonts and colours in one design
- Forgetting the phone number and M-Pesa line on every graphic

- Editing the same design from scratch instead of duplicating templates
