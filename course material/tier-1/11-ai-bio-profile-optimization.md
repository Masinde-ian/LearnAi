# AI for Instagram/TikTok Bio & Profile Optimization

**Overview**

Your bio is your shop window — visitors decide in seconds whether to follow, buy, or scroll away. This course shows you how to write a bio that converts, choose a niche and content angle with AI's help, and fix your profile picture and highlights. It ends with a 5-minute audit you can run on any profile.

**Who this is for**

- Creators and small sellers whose profile visits aren't becoming followers
- Anyone starting a page and unsure what to say about themselves
- Business owners who want their Instagram/TikTok to look professional

**What you'll be able to do**

- Write a bio that turns visitors into followers and customers
- Choose a clear niche and content angle with AI
- Get quick profile picture and highlight cover ideas
- Run a 5-minute profile audit checklist

## Lesson 1: Write a bio that converts

A good bio answers three questions in seconds:

1. **Who are you?** — name or brand
2. **What do you offer?** — product, content, or service
3. **What should I do?** — follow, DM, buy, link in bio

On Instagram you get 150 characters. On TikTok similar. Every word counts. AI drafts options fast; you pick the best.

**Do this:** include a clear call to action — "DM to order", "Follow for daily tips", "WhatsApp link in bio."

**Not this:** a bio that describes your hobbies but not what you offer. Visitors don't know what to do, so they leave.

## Lesson 2: Choose your niche and content angle

A niche is who you serve; an angle is how you talk to them. Both help AI write better content for you.

- Broad: "I post about money"
- Specific: "I help Kenyan students save on a tight budget"

Specific beats broad. With a clear angle, AI can generate captions, topics, and hooks that fit one audience — instead of generic advice that fits no one.

**Do this:** answer "who is this for?" in one line. If you can't, neither can AI.

**Not this:** posting mixed content — motivation, memes, products — with no consistent angle.

## Lesson 3: Profile picture and highlight covers

Your profile picture is your first impression. For personal brands, a clear face photo or a simple logo works best. For product pages, your best product photo.

Highlight covers organize your story highlights (prices, reviews, delivery, how to order). Canva's free version has templates for profile frames and highlight icons.

**Do this:** make all highlight covers the same style and colours — it instantly looks more professional.

**Not this:** random emoji icons or screenshots as highlight covers.

## Lesson 4: The 5-minute profile audit

Run this on your own profile, or any competitor's:

1. Open the profile as a stranger would.
2. Read the bio aloud — can you say what they do in 3 seconds?
3. Check the profile picture is clear and on-brand.
4. Count how many posts in the last month fit one niche.
5. Check the last 3 captions — do they have a call to action?
6. Is the link in bio working and up to date?
7. Note one fix you'll make today.

**Do this:** audit once a month. Small fixes compound.

## Prompt Library

Copy, fill in the blanks, and adapt.

**Bio from scratch**

Use to generate bio options when you're stuck.

```
Write 5 Instagram bio options for [me/my business] who [what you do, e.g. sells handmade beaded sandals from Mombasa]. Max 150 characters each. Each bio must say who I am, what I offer, and a call to action (DM to order, link in bio). Kenyan context, no emojis.
```

**Bio with a call to action**

Use when your bio describes but doesn't convert.

```
My current bio is: [paste bio]. Rewrite it so a visitor knows exactly what to do within 3 seconds: follow, DM, or buy. Keep it under 150 characters and add a clear call to action.
```

**Niche and angle finder**

Use to sharpen who you serve.

```
I'm starting a page about [topic, e.g. budgeting]. Suggest 5 specific niches within it for Kenya, each with a clear audience (e.g. university students, small business owners). Then recommend the best niche for me if I [your situation, e.g. work in a salon and sell cosmetics]. Give each niche a one-line content angle.
```

**Content pillars from your niche**

Use to know what to post.

```
For a page in the niche "[niche]", give me 4 content pillars (recurring post topics) and 3 example post ideas for each. Kenyan audience, practical and specific. No emojis.
```

**Profile picture ideas**

Use when you're unsure what to use.

```
Suggest 5 profile picture options for [type: personal brand / product page / service business] in [industry, e.g. beauty]. Explain why each works and what to avoid. I can shoot with a phone camera, no professional photoshoot.
```

**Highlight cover categories**

Use to organize your story highlights.

```
Suggest 6 story highlight names and short cover-text labels for a page that [what you do, e.g. sells Kenyan coffee and delivers countrywide]. Examples: Prices, Delivery, Reviews. Keep each label 1-2 words.
```

**Audit my bio with a fresh eye**

Use to catch what you can't see yourself.

```
Here is my current bio: [paste bio]. Acting as a first-time visitor, tell me in 3 bullet points what's unclear, and rewrite the bio to fix it. Keep facts the same.
```

**Link in bio text**

Use when you have a link page or WhatsApp link.

```
Write the text for my link-in-bio page for [offer, e.g. my catalog and booking form]. Short labels for: WhatsApp ordering, catalog, delivery info, and reviews. Kenyan tone, no emojis.
```

**Competitor profile teardown**

Use to study pages doing better than yours.

```
Analyze this competitor bio and profile: [paste their bio]. What is their niche, their call to action, and their angle? What can I borrow without copying, for my page about [your niche]?
```

**Content angle statement**

Use as a one-line guide for all your posts.

```
Write a one-sentence content angle for my page: I help [audience] with [problem] through [type of content] in a [tone] way. Example: I help Kenyan students save on a tight budget through weekly tips in a friendly, no-jargon tone. Fill it in for me based on: [your details]
```

## Action plan

1. Pick one niche and one content angle using the prompts.
2. Write 3 bio options, then choose and update your bio.
3. Fix your profile picture or pick a new one.
4. Create highlight covers in one consistent style.
5. Run the 5-minute audit on your own profile.
6. Make the one fix that matters most today.
7. Post 3 pieces of content that match your angle this week.

- [ ] Link in bio works
- [ ] I completed the 5-minute audit

## Common mistakes to avoid

- A bio with no call to action
- Posting without a niche or consistent angle
- Emoji-filled bios that say nothing about what you offer
- Profile pictures that are blurry or cropped badly
- Broken or outdated link in bio
- Copying a competitor's bio word-for-word
