# AI for Naming Your Business & Writing a Slogan

**Overview**

A good name makes your business easy to remember, easy to find, and easy to trust. This course uses AI to generate and shortlist name ideas, check if they're taken, and pair them with a slogan that's clear and memorable. You'll also learn the basics of registering and protecting your name in Kenya.

**Who this is for**

- Anyone about to start a business and stuck on a name
- Existing businesses with a name that's hard to say, spell, or find online
- Sellers who want a slogan that customers repeat

**What you'll be able to do**

- Generate and shortlist business name ideas with AI
- Check name availability on domains and social handles quickly
- Write a slogan that is memorable and clear
- Understand basic trademark and registration steps in Kenya

## Lesson 1: Generate name ideas with AI

A good business name is:

- Short and easy to say in English or Kiswahili
- Easy to spell when someone searches it
- Not already taken by a big brand in the same field

AI can produce hundreds of name ideas in minutes. The skill is telling it what to focus on and shortlisting honestly.

Give AI: what you sell, who you serve, and the feeling you want (modern, local, trustworthy, premium). Ask for names that are easy to spell and can work as a WhatsApp handle.

**Do this:** ask for 20–30 names, then cut it to your top 5 by saying each aloud to someone.

**Not this:** picking the first catchy option without checking it's available.

## Lesson 2: Check availability fast

Before you fall in love with a name, check:

- **Social handles** — search the name on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and X
- **Domain** — check `name.co.ke` and `.com` on any domain registrar
- **Google** — see if another Kenyan business already uses it
- **Trademark register** — search via the Kenya Industrial Property Institute (KIPI)

AI can't check availability for you — but it can suggest spelling variations that are free when your first choice is taken.

**Do this:** get the exact handle for the name on the main platform where you sell. A name you can't use on WhatsApp or Instagram is a name you don't want.

**Not this:** launching with a name that's taken on the one platform your customers use.

## Lesson 3: Write a memorable slogan

A slogan is one short sentence that says what you do and why it matters. The best ones are clear first, clever second.

- "Fresh from the farm to your table" — says what and why
- "Every day affordable" — promise in three words
- "Your hair, done right" — speaks to the customer directly

AI generates dozens of options. Edit them hard — cut every word that doesn't earn its place.

**Do this:** read the slogan aloud. If you have to explain it, it's not ready.

**Not this:** a slogan full of buzzwords ("synergy", "solutions") that means nothing.

## Lesson 4: Registration basics in Kenya

Registration gives you a legal identity and helps protect your name. The basics:

- Register the business name via the **Business Registration Service (BRS)** on eCitizen
- Sole proprietors can register a business name; companies are separate and more formal
- For real legal protection of a brand name, consider a **trademark** through KIPI
- Your M-Pesa business till or paybill name should match your registered name to build trust

AI can help you draft a business description for registration and remind you of documents, but only you can file and pay the government fees.

**Do this:** register early. An unregistered name can be taken by someone else, and banks, wholesalers, and M-Pesa accounts are easier to open with a registered name.

**Not this:** skipping registration until "the business is big" — by then the name you love may be gone.

## Prompt Library

Copy, fill in the blanks, and adapt.

**Name ideas from your business**

Use at the start.

```
I'm starting a business that [what you do, e.g. sells homemade peanut butter in Nairobi] targeting [audience, e.g. working mums]. Give me 25 name ideas that are short, easy to spell, and work well for a WhatsApp business profile. Mix English and Kiswahili where natural. No emojis.
```

**Name with a specific feeling**

Use when you want a certain tone.

```
Give me 15 business name ideas for a [business type] that feels [modern / local / trustworthy / premium]. The business [what you do]. Each name under 3 words and easy to say aloud.
```

**Shortlist my top 5**

Use to choose between your favourites.

```
Here are my 5 shortlisted business names: [list]. Compare them on: ease of spelling, how they sound in English and Kiswahili, and whether they clearly say what I do. Recommend one and explain why in 3 sentences.
```

**Variations when the name is taken**

Use when your first choice is unavailable.

```
The name "[name]" is already taken on social media. Suggest 10 similar variations that keep the same feel but are likely to be available, including different spellings and adding words like "Hub", "Store", "Ke", or "Soko".
```

**Checklist before choosing**

Use before you commit to a name.

```
Give me a 6-point checklist to check before finalizing a business name: social media handles, domain, Google search, trademark, how it sounds aloud, and any local meanings. Keep each point to one line.
```

**Slogan from a one-line description**

Use to build slogan options.

```
Write 12 slogan options for [business name], a business that [what you do, e.g. delivers fresh vegetables in Eldoret]. Each slogan under 7 words, clear first and clever second. No emojis.
```

**Slogan that names the customer**

Use to speak directly to your buyer.

```
Write 8 slogans for [business name] that speak directly to the customer (use "you" or "your"). The customer is [audience]. Example style: "Your hair, done right."
```

**Polish my chosen slogan**

Use to tighten your pick.

```
My chosen slogan is: "[slogan]". Make it shorter and stronger without losing the meaning. Give me 3 tightened versions and one recommendation.
```

**Business description for registration**

Use when filling BRS/eCitizen forms.

```
Write a short business description for [business name], a [business type] in [location] that [what you do]. Keep it factual, 2-3 sentences, suitable for a registration or bank account application. No hype.
```

**FAQ answers about registration**

Use to understand what's needed before you start.

```
Explain in simple terms: how to register a business name in Kenya via the Business Registration Service on eCitizen, what a trademark via KIPI protects, and whether a small seller needs both. Answer like you're explaining to a friend. Keep it under 200 words.
```

## Action plan

1. Generate 25–30 name ideas with AI.
2. Shortlist to 5 and say each aloud to a friend.
3. Check availability: social handles, domain, Google.
4. If your top pick is taken, run the variations prompt.
5. Write a slogan and tighten it with AI.
6. Register the name via BRS on eCitizen.
7. Update your WhatsApp Business, social handles, and M-Pesa till name to match.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Choosing a name before checking availability
- Picking something too similar to an established Kenyan brand
- A slogan that needs explaining
- Skipping registration until it's too late
- Using trendy spellings that are hard to type or search
- Falling in love with a name before the checks
