# Social Media Content System: 30 Days of Content in 2 Hours

## Overview

Posting daily is a habit; posting daily for a month is a system. This course shows you how to plan a 30-day content calendar, batch-generate captions and hooks by theme, repurpose one piece of content into five formats, and read simple analytics so you keep what works and drop what does not. One focused session runs your whole month.

## Who this is for

- Small business owners who want to post consistently without burnout
- Content creators and personal brands growing a following
- Social media managers who handle client accounts
- Anyone posting on and off and wondering why nothing sticks

## What you'll be able to do

- Plan a full month of content around clear themes
- Batch-generate captions, hooks, and ideas in one session
- Repurpose one piece of content into five formats with AI
- Track simple analytics and decide what to double down on
- Run your entire month of content in about two hours

## Lesson 1: The two-hour monthly system

Consistency is what grows accounts, but daily creation burns people out. The fix is to create once for the whole month: one session of about two hours where you plan the calendar, write the captions, and batch the designs.

The system has four steps: set your themes, build the 30-day calendar, batch the content by theme, then repurpose and schedule. Every month you repeat the same four steps, faster than the last.

Keep your business, tone, and audience fixed in every prompt so all thirty days stay on brand. You are not posting thirty separate ideas; you are repeating a few strong themes thirty times.

## Lesson 2: Define your content pillars

A content pillar is a theme you talk about regularly. Pick three to five pillars for your page — for a salon: services, results and transformations, tips, and behind-the-scenes. Every post belongs to a pillar.

Pillars keep you from posting random things. When you know your pillars, generating a month of ideas is easy: each pillar gives you roughly seven to ten posts per month.

Mix the pillars so the month is balanced: mostly value and tips, some behind-the-scenes, and a smaller slice of direct selling. Too many sales posts push followers away; too many tips never sell anything.

## Lesson 3: Build the 30-day calendar

With pillars in place, ask AI for a 30-day calendar. Give it your pillars, your platform, and the mix you want, and it will map a day-by-day plan with a topic for each post.

Set the mix up front: about 60 percent value and tips, 20 percent engagement and questions, 20 percent sales and offers. Add local hooks — public holidays, school calendars, election-adjacent quiet periods — so the month feels alive, not generic.

You are not committing to the calendar forever. It is a plan you adjust weekly. The point is to never sit in front of a blank caption box again.

## Lesson 4: Batch-generating captions, hooks, and ideas

Batching means writing everything in one sitting. Run one prompt per pillar asking for a full batch of captions, each with a strong hook line, the body, and a call to action.

A hook is the first line that decides whether someone keeps reading or scrolls. Generate ten hook variations for your topic and reuse the winners. Over weeks you build a hook library that works for your audience.

Batch with the same platform and tone in the prompt so nothing needs rework. Pull the best options, tweak a few, and save each day's post in your notes app in order.

## Lesson 5: Repurpose one piece into five formats

You do not need thirty original ideas. One strong post can become five formats: the original post, a carousel breaking the idea into slides, a short video script, a WhatsApp Status line, and a LinkedIn or email version.

Run the repurposing prompt below on your best content of the week. The AI keeps the core idea and reshapes it for each platform's style — shorter for TikTok, conversational for WhatsApp, professional for LinkedIn.

Repurposing triples your output with the same effort. Choose one strong piece per week, turn it into five formats, and your month covers itself.

## Lesson 6: Simple analytics — what works, what to double down

Analytics on phone apps is simpler than it looks. On each platform, open the insights for a post and look at four numbers: reach, saves, shares, and follows.

Reach is how many people saw the post. Saves and shares show value — people acting, not just scrolling. Follows show which content grows your account. A post with high saves but low reach means your topic is good but your hook needs work. A post with high reach but no saves means the hook worked and the content let it down.

Read the numbers in pairs, not alone. Compare a value post against a sales post and you will see that the value post builds the audience that eventually buys. Look for patterns across the month, not one lucky post.

Review once a month: keep posting what gets saves and shares, improve hooks on posts that get reach but no action, and quietly retire topics that do neither. Double down on your winners, not your guesses. A monthly 15-minute review is all it takes to steer the whole system.

## Lesson 7: Batch the visuals in Canva

Text is half the post; the image gets the thumb to stop. Batch your visuals in the same two-hour session using Canva templates you already own.

Build one template per pillar with your brand colours and font, then duplicate it for each post and swap the text. A salon posts a tips template, a results template, and a sales template — thirty posts become thirty quick edits.

Keep the design system light: two fonts, three colours, one consistent layout. Fast batching beats perfect design when you are doing thirty posts at once.

## Lesson 8: Post, schedule, and keep the rhythm

The system finishes where posting begins. Schedule your batched content with Meta Business Suite for Facebook and Instagram, or post manually in a daily two-minute routine. Scheduling removes the daily decision entirely.

Posting times matter less than consistency. Morning and evening commute hours in Kenya often work well, but your own analytics will tell you when your audience is awake. Check the insights tab for when your followers are online, then schedule into those slots.

Follow the monthly rhythm: plan and batch in one session, post daily, review analytics at month end, adjust. A two-hour session becomes the whole month's content work, and the daily habit keeps the account alive between sessions.

## Prompt Library

Copy, fill the brackets, and reuse every month.

**Content pillars**

```
I run a [business] targeting [audience] in [town]. Suggest 4 content
pillars I can post about every month. For each pillar give: the name,
3 example topics, and the type of post (value, engagement, or sales).
```

How to use: run once to define your pillars, then reuse them monthly.

**30-day calendar**

```
Build a 30-day content calendar for my [business] page on [platform].
Pillars: [pillars]. Mix: 60% value, 20% engagement, 20% sales. Add
one line per day: the topic and the pillar. Space out sales posts.
```

How to use: run monthly, then adjust around local holidays and events.

**Weekly caption batch**

```
Write 7 captions for next week for my [business] on [platform].
Topics: [list 7 topics]. Same tone: [tone]. Each with a hook line,
a body, and one call to action. No emojis.
```

How to use: run once a week or once a month for the full calendar.

**Hook variations**

```
Give me 10 different opening hooks for a post about [topic] on
[platform]. Styles: question, bold claim, story, curiosity gap, and
warning. One line each.
```

How to use: pick the best three and rotate them across the month.

**Carousel outline**

```
Turn this post into a 6-slide carousel. Slide 1: hook title. Slides
2-5: one key point each. Slide 6: call to action. Keep each slide
under 15 words: [paste post]
```

How to use: repurpose your best value post into a carousel.

**Short video script**

```
Write a 30-second script for a TikTok/Reel on [topic]. Start with a
hook line, then the message in 3 short sentences, end with a call
to action. Include a visual note for each line.
```

How to use: pair with your own footage or stock clips.

**WhatsApp Status line**

```
Write a WhatsApp Status line (max 2 lines) that shares [topic]
and ends with "DM to learn more". Conversational, warm.
```

How to use: repurpose the same idea for Status viewers.

**LinkedIn/email version**

```
Rewrite this post as a professional LinkedIn post and as a short
email version. Keep the core idea, adjust the tone, add a clear
next step: [paste post]
```

How to use: extend one idea across LinkedIn and your list.

**Engagement question**

```
Write 5 engagement questions my audience would actually answer about
[niche/topic]. Simple, specific, low effort to reply to. One line each.
```

How to use: slot one into your engagement days on the calendar.

**Hashtag set by pillar**

```
Give me 10 hashtags for an Instagram post about [pillar/topic] in
Kenya. Mix 4 broad, 4 mid, 2 specific. Comma-separated, no spaces.
```

How to use: reuse a set per pillar instead of new hashtags each time.

**Sales post without sounding salesy**

```
Write a sales post for my [offer] at KES [amount]. Frame it around
the problem it solves and one result, not features. End with one
clear call to action. No pressure, no emojis.
```

How to use: for the 20% of the month that sells directly.

**Monthly review**

```
Here are my post topics and their numbers: reach, saves, shares,
follows. Tell me which 3 topics to double down on, which hooks to
improve, and which topics to drop: [paste data]
```

How to use: run monthly with your insights copied into the prompt.

**Fix an underperforming post**

```
This post got low engagement. Rewrite the hook in 3 stronger ways
and give 2 reasons the topic might have flopped:
[paste post and its numbers]
```

How to use: diagnose weak posts instead of guessing.

**Repurpose testimonial**

```
Turn this customer review into a social post with a short thank-you
line and a call to action to try my [service]: [paste review]
```

How to use: never waste a good testimonial; make it a post.

5. Pick one strong piece weekly and repurpose it into five formats.
6. Schedule the month and post daily.
7. Review analytics at month end and adjust the next month.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Posting random topics with no pillars
- Posting the same thing on every platform without adapting
- 90% sales posts and no value
- Ignoring analytics and guessing what works
- Starting from scratch every day instead of batching monthly
- Never repurposing — creating 30 original ideas instead of 6
