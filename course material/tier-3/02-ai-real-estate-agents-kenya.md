# AI for Real Estate Agents in Kenya

**Overview** — Kenyan property buyers and tenants decide in seconds, and the agent who responds fastest wins. This course gives you a full AI-powered marketing and sales system: listings that sell, flyers and social posts at scale, virtual tour scripts, automated inquiry handling, and market research clients trust. The one clear win: a repeatable listing-to-close pipeline that handles ten listings at once instead of one at a time.

**Who this is for**

- Independent agents managing their own listings and leads
- Agents at Kenyan agencies who want to close more, faster
- Landlords and developers marketing their own properties
- Anyone entering real estate who wants a professional system from day one

**What you'll be able to do**

- Write property listings that highlight features buyers and tenants actually want
- Produce flyers, social posts, and ad copy in minutes with AI
- Create virtual tour scripts that sell without being salesy
- Automate inquiries and follow-ups so no lead goes cold
- Build market reports and comparables clients will trust
- Keep the human, trusted touch while AI works behind the scenes
- Manage many listings at once with a repeatable system

## Module 1: Set up your agent foundation

Real estate in Kenya is a trust business. Start by making your operation legit: agents register with the Estate Agents Registration Board (EARB). Registration signals to clients — and to portals — that you are a professional, not a hustler. Get your paperwork in order early.

Then set up your tools: WhatsApp Business (your main line — most Kenyan clients message before they call), a simple CRM spreadsheet or free tool to track every listing and lead, and a Google Drive folder per property for photos, documents, and plans.

Choose a position. Are you the satellite-town specialist, the Karen mansion agent, or the commercial-let expert? A clear focus makes your marketing sharper and your market reports credible. "I sell homes in Kitengela and Syokimau" beats "I sell everything."

Set up profiles on the major Kenyan property portals — BuyRentKenya, Property24, Justhomes — and treat classifieds like Jiji and Pigiame as volume channels, knowing they carry unverified listings. Your AI-assisted professional listings will stand out in all of them.

**Do:** register with EARB and keep your paperwork visible to clients.
**Not this:** posting properties with no follow-up system and losing serious buyers to silence.

## Module 2: Write listings that sell

A listing is a sales letter, not an inventory. Tenants and buyers scroll fast, so open with the strongest selling point: the view, the location, the security, the price relative to the area. Then paint the living experience — not a dry list of rooms.

Lead with the numbers that matter. Kenyan renters care about rent, deposit, location, security, and parking. Use current market anchors honestly: suburban rents in prime areas reached around KES 201,832 a month in Q1 2026, while satellite towns average closer to KES 64,765. If your property is a 3-bedroom in Syokimau, say what that gets them for KES 65K — space, quiet, and quick access to the expressway.

Use the "so what" trick. For every feature, write the benefit: "High ceilings" becomes "High ceilings keep the house cool through the Nairobi heat." AI drafts the structure; you supply the true details — plot size, view, furnishing, what is included in the rent.

Write for the specific buyer. A family listing talks about schools and compound safety; a young professional listing talks about internet, gym access, and proximity to Westlands. One listing template per audience, personalized each time.

**Template — listing formula:**

```
HEADLINE: [Price] - [Type] in [Area] with [best feature]
3 BEDROOM APARTMENT, KITENGELA - KES [RENT], SAFE & FINISHED

Intro line: [best selling point in one sentence]
The space: [2 lines on rooms, layout, finishing]
The area: [2 lines - security, roads, schools, transport]
What's included: [parking, water, solar, fitted kitchen]
Details: [deposit, terms, availability]
Contact: [WhatsApp number] - respond within [X] hours
```

## Module 3: Marketing content at scale

One listing can feed a week of content: one portal description, one WhatsApp caption, three Instagram or TikTok captions, one Facebook post, and one flyer. AI writes the base versions; you keep the facts accurate and the tone yours.

Feed AI a property fact sheet — area, size, price, features, photos description — and ask for five different captions. Different platforms need different voices: Instagram is short and visual, Facebook can be longer and local, WhatsApp status is urgent and casual.

Design flyers in Canva in under ten minutes. Use the Magic Studio tools to generate background ideas, then drop in the real photo, price, and your contact. A clean flyer with one strong photo and a QR code to your WhatsApp beats a crowded one.

Market the area, not just the property. A "What KES 65K gets you in Syokimau" post builds authority and pulls in leads for other listings. Area posts are the content that makes you the go-to agent before clients even look for a house.

**Do:** batch a full content pack the day a listing goes live.
**Not this:** posting the same one-line caption on every platform and wondering why nobody bites.

## Module 4: Virtual tours and listing media

The first viewing is won before the client steps in. A good virtual tour shortlists your property and filters out time-wasters. You do not need a drone or a studio — a phone, good light, and a script work.

Shoot the tour in a logical walk: gate and security, parking, entrance, living space, kitchen, bedrooms, ensuite, outdoor. Hold the phone steady, move slowly, and talk in short sentences. CapCut edits it into a tight 60-90 second video with captions — captions matter because many Kenyans scroll with sound off.

Write the tour script with AI: a calm walk-through narration that highlights the selling points at the moment they appear on screen. Record the voiceover in your own voice if you can; your voice builds the trust a text-to-speech voice never will.

Pair the video with 5-8 still photos shot in good daylight, plus one shot of the neighbourhood gate and the local shops. The complete package — photos, video, map link, and WhatsApp — is what makes an inquiry feel serious.

**Script structure:** open with area + price, walk the rooms in order, name one benefit per room, close with what makes this one different and your WhatsApp number.

## Module 5: Automate inquiries and follow-ups

Most Kenyan agents lose deals to silence. A serious client messages at 9pm; an agent replies at 9am and the client has booked elsewhere. Automation closes that gap.

Set up WhatsApp Business with quick replies for the questions you get ten times a day: rent, deposit, parking, availability, viewing times. A one-tap reply in seconds makes you look fast and organized.

Write an auto-reply for outside working hours: "Asante for your message. I am with clients but will respond within 2 hours. Meanwhile, here are the key details: [rent, deposit, area, viewing slots]." The client stays warm while you sleep.

Use a simple follow-up ladder: reply within minutes, confirm a viewing within 24 hours, remind the day before the viewing, follow up the day after. AI drafts all of these; a spreadsheet or your WhatsApp labels tell you who is where in the ladder.

**Follow-up ladder template:**

```
1. [Within 15 min] Reply with price, area, key details, and two
   viewing slots. Ask one question: "Are you looking to move this
   month?"
2. [Within 24 hrs] Confirm the viewing: date, time, location pin,
   what to bring (ID, payslip).
3. [Day before viewing] Reminder + "call me if the time changes".
4. [Day after viewing] Thank them + one genuine question about the
   house and one alternative property if they are hesitating.
```

Speed is the strategy. Fast, warm, consistent replies turn interest into viewings, and viewings into offers.

## Module 6: Market research and comparables

Clients trust the agent who knows what things actually rent for. AI turns scattered market data into a clear picture — but only you can verify it. Use it to draft, then confirm with real portal listings and your own viewings.

Build a comparables sheet: for any property, gather 5-8 similar homes in the same area, their rent or price, size, and condition. This sheet is your pricing bible. It answers "why is this KES 70K and the next one 60K?" with facts, not feelings.

Ask AI to analyse the numbers you collected: where is your price fair, where is it high, and how to present the property against the market. Use the 2026 picture to guide expectations — prime suburban rents are high and holding, while oversupplied apartment towers in Westlands and Upper Hill need sharper pricing and stronger positioning.

Write a short market report for every serious client: area overview, average rents by type, recent trends, and what their money gets them. AI drafts the structure; you add the local truth only a real agent knows. A one-page report turns a viewing into a relationship.

**Do:** check the rental yield picture too — gross yields around 7.4% guide investors.
**Not this:** quoting made-up "market value" that breaks down the moment the client does their own research.

## Module 7: Build trust while AI works behind the scenes

AI can write your listing and draft your follow-up, but the client must believe a human is in charge. The rule: AI in the back office, you in the front line. Never paste AI output into a client chat without reading and personalizing it.

Transparency is a feature, not a risk. If a client asks, tell them simply: "I use tools to write listings and research the market so I can respond to you faster." Clients care about speed and accuracy, not about which tool produced the words — they care that the facts are right.

Keep the human moments human: the site visit, the negotiation, the handshake, the honest answer about a damp wall or a noisy neighbour. AI never replaces your local knowledge or your reputation; it multiplies your output so you have more time for those moments.

Guard your reputation on portals too. Jiji and Pigiame carry unverified listings — post clean, honest, verified details on the professional portals and let your consistency build your name. A client who meets a straight-talking agent is a client who refers you.

**Trust rule:** every fact AI writes for you gets checked against the real property before it is published. One wrong photo or wrong price erases a hundred good captions.

## Prompt Library

**Listing writer**

```
Write a property listing for: [TYPE, AREA, SIZE, PRICE/RENT, TOP
FEATURES]. Open with the strongest selling point, describe the
living experience, add an "what's included" line, and end with a
call to message me on WhatsApp. Kenyan market, no hype, under 200
words. Facts I gave you are the only facts you may use.
```

How to use it: run per property, then check every fact before posting.

**Headline generator**

```
Create 10 headlines for this listing: [PROPERTY DETAILS]. Mix
price-first, area-first, and feature-first styles. Each under 90
characters, suitable for [PORTAL NAME]. Mark the 3 strongest.
```

How to use it: pick the best headline and rotate the rest as A/B tests.

**Platform-adapted captions**

```
Write 5 short captions for this listing: [DETAILS]. One for
Instagram, one for Facebook, one for WhatsApp status, one for
TikTok, and one for a classified site like Jiji. Match each
platform's tone and length. No emojis, no cliches.
```

How to use it: post the full pack the day a listing goes live.

**Area selling post**

```
Write a social post educating buyers/tenants about [AREA] in Kenya.
Topic: what [KES/AMOUNT] gets you in [AREA] this year — space,
security, commute, schools. 3 facts I give you, [VERIFY BEFORE
POSTING], tone warm and expert, under 150 words, ends with an open
question.
```

How to use it: build authority between listings and attract area-specific leads.

**Flyer copy**

```
Write the text for a property flyer for [DETAILS]: headline, 3
feature bullets, one line on the area, and a clear contact line
with WhatsApp. Space for a photo and a QR code to WhatsApp. Under
60 words, bold and clean.
```

How to use it: paste into Canva and drop in the real photo.

**Virtual tour script**

```
Write a 60-90 second voiceover script for a video tour of
[PROPERTY DETAILS]. Walk the rooms in order: gate, parking,
entrance, living area, kitchen, bedrooms, outdoor. One benefit per
room, calm and natural, close with what makes it special and my
WhatsApp number. Speakable in 90 seconds.
```

How to use it: record it yourself in your own voice over the CapCut edit.

**Viewing walk-through checklist**

```
Create a checklist for recording a video tour on a phone. Include:
time of day for best light, order of rooms, camera handling (steady,
slow), what to say in each room, and 5-8 still photos to also take.
Under 15 steps, practical for a phone.
```

How to use it: print or keep on a second phone while you shoot.

**WhatsApp quick replies**

```
Write quick replies for a real estate agent on WhatsApp Business.
Cover these 8 questions: rent/price, deposit, parking, availability
date, is it furnished, viewing times, security in the area, and
papers/documents. Each reply under 40 words, warm, ends with a
question or next step.
```

How to use it: paste each into a WhatsApp Business quick reply.

**After-hours auto-reply**

```
Write an after-hours auto-reply for a real estate agent's WhatsApp
Business. It must: thank the client, say when I will respond
(within 2 hours / next morning), give key details of [LISTING] in
one line, and offer two viewing slots. Warm, brief, under 80 words.
```

How to use it: switch it on when you are not available.

**Inquiry first reply**

```
A prospect messaged about [LISTING]: "[PASTE THEIR MESSAGE]". Write
a reply that answers their question, confirms the key details
(price, deposit, area, availability), offers two viewing times, and
asks one qualification question. Under 90 words, fast and warm.
```

How to use it: adapt and send within minutes of the message.

**Viewing confirmation**

```
Write a confirmation message for a property viewing at [DATE/TIME]
for [AREA/PROPERTY]. Include: the location pin instructions, what
to bring (ID, payslip), and "call me if the time changes". Also add
one line that makes the client feel the listing is worth their trip.
Under 70 words.
```

How to use it: send once the slot is agreed, then again the day before.

**Post-viewing follow-up**

```
A client viewed [PROPERTY] today. Write a follow-up message that:
thanks them, asks one genuine question about what they liked, and
offers one alternative property if they are unsure. Under 90 words,
no pressure, warm.
```

How to use it: send within 24 hours of every viewing.

**Comparables analysis**

```
Here are 8 comparable properties in [AREA] I collected: [PASTE
DETAILS WITH RENTS/SIZES]. Analyse them: where is my listing's
price fair, where is it high, and how should I position it against
the market? Give me a 3-sentence positioning summary and 3 facts
to use in negotiations. Note anything I should verify.
```

How to use it: run before pricing or negotiating any listing.

**Market report**

```
Write a one-page market brief for [AREA] in Kenya for a client
looking to [RENT/BUY]. Sections: area overview, average prices by
property type (use my numbers: [DATA]), trends, and what their
budget of [AMOUNT] gets them. Professional, readable on a phone,
with a "verify this" note on any claim I must confirm.
```

How to use it: send as a PDF or WhatsApp document before a serious viewing.

**Rental yield check**

```
For a property priced at [PRICE] with expected monthly rent of
[RENT], calculate gross annual rental yield and compare it to the
Kenyan average of about 7.4%. Explain what the figure means for an
investor and how to present it to the buyer. Show the working.
```

How to use it: run for investor clients who want to buy-to-let.

**Negotiation response**

```
A client is negotiating [PRICE/RENT] down to [OFFER] on [PROPERTY]
in [AREA]. Write a professional reply that acknowledges the offer,
states my position with one market fact from my comparables, and
proposes a middle point or a small concession with a condition.
Warm, firm, under 100 words.
```

How to use it: adapt with your real numbers before sending.

**Content calendar for your listings**

```
Build a 7-day content calendar for a real estate agent in [AREA]
with [NUMBER] active listings. Mix: listing posts, one area
education post, one client story, one behind-the-scenes, one video
tour. Give day, platform, caption idea, and asset needed. No emojis.
```

How to use it: batch-create all assets on Sunday, post all week.

**Client trust message**

```
A client asked how I write listings so fast. Write a short reply
explaining I use AI tools to draft and research so I can respond
and update them faster, and that all facts are personally verified
by me. Honest, confident, no jargon, under 80 words.
```

How to use it: keep ready for the transparency question without sounding robotic.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Posting listings with no follow-up system, so leads die in silence
- Copy-pasting AI captions with wrong prices or wrong photos
- Listing on unverified classifieds only, without professional portals
- Using a market report with numbers you cannot defend
- Automating replies so heavily the client feels they are chatting with a bot
- Ignoring the over-supplied segments (e.g. prime apartment towers) and pricing them blindly
- Delaying EARB registration and losing credibility with serious clients
