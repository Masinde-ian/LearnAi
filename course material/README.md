# LearnAI Course Library — Kenya

A 4-tier course ladder built for the Kenyan market: cheap entry offers (easy impulse buys), paired upsells that go deeper on the same topic, standalone niche courses for people with a specific goal, and premium flagship courses for people ready to invest seriously.

Each course lives in its tier subfolder as a standalone `.md` file, ready to be converted to PDF/EPUB or used as copy for the LearnAI site.

## Pricing tiers

| Tier | Name | Price | Count |
|------|------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Entry | KES 500 | 25 |
| 2 | Upsell | KES 2,500 | 20 |
| 3 | Niche | KES 5,000+ | 12 |
| 4 | Premium | KES 10,000+ | 8 |
| **Total** | | | **65** |

## Funnel flow

1. **Ad → Tier 1 course (KES 500)** — easy, low-risk first purchase
2. **Immediate upsell → matching Tier 2 course (KES 2,500)** right after purchase or via WhatsApp follow-up
3. **Email/WhatsApp nurture → Tier 3 niche course (KES 5,000+)** for buyers who want a specific outcome
4. **Retarget best customers → Tier 4 premium course (KES 10,000+)** once they've bought a lower tier and follow through
